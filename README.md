⚔️ AI Pokémon GO 3v3 Matchmaker

🌐 The Backstory: Built on Fan Power
    Before we could write a single line of AI code, we needed data—lots of it. That’s where the Pokémon community comes in.
    For years, dedicated fan developers have meticulously scraped and organized data from every single Pokémon game ever made. They packed all of that collective knowledge into **PokéAPI**, a massive, completely free public data server. Because these fans made their hard work public to the world, developers like us can easily connect our code to their servers and download the exact base stats and type charts for any Pokémon instantly. Using their incredible community database as our launchpad, we built this app.

🕹️ What This App Does
    Ever get tired of picking a cool Pokémon as your lead, only to get instantly swept by a random counter-type? This project solves that.
    Instead of guessing your coverage, this app hooks into that fan-made data stream and uses real **Machine Learning** to build the ultimate 3v3 team triangle for the Pokémon GO Battle League. You give it your favorite starter, and it instantly builds a flawless squad around it.

🧠 How it works under the hood:
      The AI Brain:** It analyzes the stats of all Generation 1 Pokémon to figure out who behaves like a heavy hitter, a fast shield-pressure spammer, or an absolute defensive tank.
      The No-Weakness Loop:** It runs through every single type matchup to guarantee that **no two Pokémon on your team share the same weakness.** If your lead gets countered, your backup is mathematically built to smash them.
      Epic Entry Music:** The second your perfect team is locked in, the app automatically triggers the authentic 8-bit battle screams of all three Pokémon back-to-back as they get summoned to the field.

It even spins up a private website link so you can use the AI tool directly on your phone while you climb the competitive ladder!
